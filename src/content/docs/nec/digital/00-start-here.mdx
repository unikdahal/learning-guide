This course teaches **NEC Chapter 2 — Digital Logic and Microprocessor (AExE02)** from zero, at the depth Lok Sewa Computer Engineer's written paper needs. The same material is **Lok Sewa CE unit 3 (digital design)** and **unit 2.8 (808X microprocessors)**, both in Section A.

You already think in bits as a programmer. Here you'll learn how those bits are made from gates and flip-flops, and how a microprocessor fetches, decodes and executes instructions.

## How to study each lesson (45–60 minutes)

1. Read the idea section; try the lab if there is one.
2. Work the examples with pen and paper — digital logic is learned by **drawing**: truth tables, K-maps, timing diagrams.
3. Read "Traps".
4. Do "Check yourself" without looking back.
5. If there's a **"Lok Sewa: write it"** section, write your answer by hand, timed, then compare with the model answer.
