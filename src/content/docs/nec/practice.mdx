Practise the shared computer-engineering core alongside NEC chapter topics. These **{practiceQuestions.length} original learning questions are not past-year questions (PYQs)**, an official model paper, or a prediction of exam weighting. There are two questions for each of the {practiceSections.length} syllabus sections, with 12 per chapter. This gives section coverage, not exhaustive coverage of every listed concept or official exam weighting.

Search matches question text or a section number such as `10.6`. Subject, section, search, and review filters combine; “Show all questions” clears them. Filter changes start a fresh round and retain your history.

Section 10.6 uses institutional roles and registration concepts checked against the [Nepal Engineering Council Act, 2055, consolidated Law Commission text](https://giwmscdnone.gov.np/media/app/public/275/posts/1719397746_81.pdf), especially sections 3 and 11–16. These questions avoid amendment dates, office-holder details, and penalty trivia.

Question tags follow the [official NEC Computer Engineering syllabus](https://nec.gov.np/uploads/Syllabus-Registration/1763716654201_Syllabus%20Computer%20Engineering.pdf). Use the [syllabus map](/learning-guide/nec/syllabus/) to plan broader revision. Common-core practice includes programming, organization, networks, DSA, databases, operating systems, software engineering, and AI; topic labels here refer to NEC sections rather than a separate Lok Sewa paper mapping.

Answers and bookmarks are stored only in this browser when storage is available. Restarting a round or changing filters keeps history. Clearing this site's browser data removes it. Device changes, private browsing, or blocked storage may prevent persistence. Latest mistakes means questions whose **most recent submitted answer** was incorrect; correct retries remove them from the next mistake round.
