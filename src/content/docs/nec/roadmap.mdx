**Window:** Mon 16 Nov → Fri 1 Jan (7 weeks, about 147 hours). The exam window opens Mon 4 Jan; 2–3 Jan are for travel and a light taper.

## Daily shape

You get home around 21:00, so everything runs at night.

| Time | Block | What goes here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lunch break | 15 min | 20 MCQs on the current chapter, on your phone |
| 21:00–21:45 | No study | Eat, shower, sit down |
| 21:45–23:15 | **A — deep work** (90 min) | New theory and numericals. Phone in another room. |
| 23:30–00:45 | **B — recall** (75 min) | MCQs, flashcards, error log |
| By 01:45 | Lights out | Same time every night |

**Friday** is Block B only (45 min) or a night off. **Saturday** is about 6.5 h (12:00–14:30, 16:00–18:00, 21:30–23:30). **Sunday** is about 4.5 h, with the evening off.

<Aside type="tip" title="Bad day rule">
Late office, rain on the ride, headache: do 45 minutes of Block A or 25 MCQs, then sleep. Two bad days in a row: drop the next Saturday's evening block. Don't try to repay the hours.
</Aside>

## The Lok Sewa thread

NEC comes first, but every week also builds toward the Computer Engineer paper (Computer Officer is the backup):

- **Sunday 16:00 block:** write one Lok Sewa Paper II answer (10 marks, 16 minutes, by hand) from that week's chapter, using the "Paper II questions to write" list at the bottom of each chapter page. Self-mark: definition 2, structure 1, diagram 2, depth 3, example 1, conclusion 1.
- **Cheap extras done in-week** (marked ★ below): topics on the Computer Engineer syllabus that sit next to what you're already studying for NEC.
- Everything else Lok Sewa-only (electronic communications, compiler design, emerging tech, technical writing, case studies, general awareness) starts 4 Jan. The [Computer Engineer syllabus map](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/computer-engineer/) lists it.

## Weekly review (Sunday, 15 min)

Log four numbers: hours studied, sub-chapters finished out of 60, this week's mock score, and error-log entries added and cleared. If a score is flat for two weeks, change the method (more MCQs, more repasses), not the hours.
