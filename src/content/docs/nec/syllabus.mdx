The official ACtE syllabus has 10 chapters with 6 sub-chapters each. Chapters 1–2 are fundamentals, 3–9 are applied computer engineering, and 10 (shared by every engineering discipline) is project planning and the NEC Act. Each chapter page lists every official topic.

## What the questions look like

From NEC's official model paper: mostly one-line fact recall plus very short numericals. Examples (paraphrased): the maximum efficiency of a class B push-pull stage, the two's complement of a binary number, how many NOR gates make an AND gate, ideal pipeline CPI, the Windows command for the ARP cache, the best-case complexity of merge sort, A3 sheet dimensions, the depreciation method whose charges rise each year, and what the NEC Act requires of institutions.

So the method is: learn the definitions and "know cold" facts in each chapter, drill MCQs, and keep an error log.
