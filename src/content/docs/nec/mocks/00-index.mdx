The plan has five full mocks. NEC has published only **one** official model paper, so the site supplies its own NEC-format mocks: **100 questions, 10 per chapter, every sub-chapter represented**, original wording, with every numerical checked by computation.

| Date | Mock | Source | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sat 21 Nov | Diagnostic | the **official NEC model paper** (from NEC) | baseline |
| Sat 12 Dec | [Mock 2](/learning-guide/nec/mocks/02-mock-2/) | this site | ≥ 50 (checkpoint) |
| Sat 26 Dec | Mock 3 | third-party sets (site mock to come) | ≥ 60 |
| Wed 30 Dec | Mock 4 | third-party sets (site mock to come) | ≥ 65 |
| Fri 1 Jan | Mock 5 | third-party sets (site mock to come) | ≥ 70, every chapter ≥ 6 |

<Aside type="tip" title="How to sit a mock">
1. Same conditions as the exam: one sitting, **2 hours**, no notes, phone away. Press **Start timer**.
2. First pass: answer everything you know cold. Second pass: the rest. With no negative marking (see [exam format](/learning-guide/nec/exam/)), **never leave a blank**.
3. Submit. The page marks it, shows the score per chapter and opens an explanation under every question.
4. Log every miss in your error log by chapter and sub-chapter (the grey tag before each question). A chapter under 6/10 gets a repass that week.
5. Answers are saved in this browser; **Clear all answers** before a re-sit — and leave at least a week before re-sitting so you don't just remember the answers.
</Aside>
