**How to use:** 15 questions, 15 minutes, no notes. Target **12/15**. Then write the Lok Sewa 5-mark Thevenin answer from [lesson 10](/learning-guide/nec/electronics/10-thevenin/) by hand in 8 minutes.

1. Superposition theorem is applicable to:
   (a) linear networks only (b) non-linear networks only (c) all networks (d) networks with a single source
2. In superposition, the current source not acting is replaced by:
   (a) a short circuit (b) an open circuit (c) its internal voltage (d) a resistor equal to the load
3. Which quantity cannot be found directly by superposition?
   (a) branch current (b) node voltage (c) power (d) terminal voltage
4. Thevenin resistance is found by:
   (a) shorting the load (b) removing the load and killing all independent sources (c) shorting all resistors (d) opening all voltage sources
5. Vth equals:
   (a) the short-circuit current × load (b) the open-circuit voltage at the terminals (c) the source EMF always (d) zero
6. 30 V in series with 10 Ω, with 20 Ω across the terminals. Vth is:
   (a) 30 V (b) 20 V (c) 10 V (d) 15 V
7. For question 6, Rth is:
   (a) 30 Ω (b) 6.67 Ω (c) 10 Ω (d) 20 Ω
8. With Vth = 12 V and Rth = 4 Ω, the current in an 8 Ω load is:
   (a) 1 A (b) 1.5 A (c) 3 A (d) 0.5 A
9. The Norton current of a network with Vth = 15 V, Rth = 5 Ω is:
   (a) 75 A (b) 3 A (c) 0.33 A (d) 10 A
10. Norton's equivalent consists of a current source with a resistance in:
    (a) series (b) parallel (c) series–parallel (d) star
11. A 6 A current source in parallel with 4 Ω converts to a voltage source of:
    (a) 24 V in series with 4 Ω (b) 1.5 V in series with 4 Ω (c) 24 V in parallel with 4 Ω (d) 6 V in series with 4 Ω
12. Maximum power transfer occurs when the load resistance is:
    (a) zero (b) infinite (c) equal to the source resistance (d) half the source resistance
13. Efficiency at maximum power transfer is:
    (a) 25% (b) 50% (c) 75% (d) 100%
14. Vth = 10 V, Rth = 2.5 Ω. Maximum power to the load is:
    (a) 40 W (b) 20 W (c) 10 W (d) 4 W
15. For AC networks, maximum power transfer requires ZL to be:
    (a) Zth (b) the complex conjugate of Zth (c) purely resistive (d) infinite
