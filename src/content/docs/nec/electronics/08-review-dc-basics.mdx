**How to use:** 20 questions, 20 minutes, no notes. Then open the answers and log every miss with the lesson it came from. Target: **16/20** before moving to network theorems.

1. The SI unit of charge is the:
   (a) ampere (b) coulomb (c) volt (d) joule
2. 1 volt is equal to:
   (a) 1 J/s (b) 1 C/s (c) 1 J/C (d) 1 W/A²
3. 600 C passes a point in 5 minutes. The current is:
   (a) 120 A (b) 2 A (c) 0.5 A (d) 3,000 A
4. A copper wire is replaced by one of the same material, double the length and double the diameter. Its resistance becomes:
   (a) double (b) half (c) four times (d) the same
5. Which material has the lowest resistivity?
   (a) copper (b) aluminium (c) silver (d) gold
6. A 2 kΩ resistor carries 3 mA. The voltage across it is:
   (a) 0.6 V (b) 6 V (c) 60 V (d) 6,000 V
7. A heater is rated 1,000 W at 250 V. Its resistance is:
   (a) 4 Ω (b) 62.5 Ω (c) 250 Ω (d) 0.25 Ω
8. The current through a fixed resistor is doubled. The power becomes:
   (a) double (b) half (c) four times (d) unchanged
9. A 1.5 kW iron is used for 2 hours a day for 30 days. Energy used:
   (a) 45 kWh (b) 90 kWh (c) 3 kWh (d) 900 kWh
10. Resistors of 5, 10 and 15 Ω in series carry 2 A. The supply voltage is:
    (a) 30 V (b) 60 V (c) 15 V (d) 120 V
11. 20 Ω and 30 Ω in parallel give:
    (a) 50 Ω (b) 25 Ω (c) 12 Ω (d) 10 Ω
12. Four 8 Ω resistors in parallel, then that combination in series with 3 Ω. Total:
    (a) 35 Ω (b) 5 Ω (c) 11 Ω (d) 2 Ω
13. In a parallel circuit, which is the same for every branch?
    (a) current (b) resistance (c) voltage (d) power
14. 30 V is applied across 1 kΩ and 2 kΩ in series. Voltage across the 2 kΩ:
    (a) 10 V (b) 15 V (c) 20 V (d) 30 V
15. At a node, 4 A and 6 A enter, and I1 and 3 A leave. I1 is:
    (a) 13 A (b) 7 A (c) 1 A (d) 10 A
16. The algebraic sum of voltages around a closed loop is zero. This is:
    (a) KCL, from conservation of charge (b) KVL, from conservation of energy (c) Ohm's law (d) Thevenin's theorem
17. A delta of three 12 Ω resistors converts to a star of:
    (a) 36 Ω each (b) 12 Ω each (c) 6 Ω each (d) 4 Ω each
18. Which element is non-linear?
    (a) resistor (b) inductor (c) diode (d) capacitor
19. Which element is unilateral?
    (a) resistor (b) diode (c) capacitor (d) inductor
20. An ideal current source has an internal resistance of:
    (a) zero (b) infinite (c) 1 Ω (d) equal to the load

<details>
<summary>Answers and reasons</summary>

1. **(b)** coulomb.
2. **(c)** volt = joule per coulomb.
3. **(b)** 600 C ÷ 300 s = 2 A.
4. **(b)** Double the diameter → area × 4 → R ÷ 4; double the length → R × 2. Net ÷ 2.
5. **(c)** silver.
6. **(b)** 2 kΩ × 3 mA = 6 V.
7. **(b)** R = V²/P = 62,500/1,000 = 62.5 Ω.
8. **(c)** P = I²R.
9. **(b)** 1.5 × 2 × 30 = 90 kWh.
10. **(b)** 30 Ω × 2 A = 60 V.
11. **(c)** 600/50 = 12 Ω.
12. **(b)** 8/4 = 2 Ω, plus 3 Ω = 5 Ω.
13. **(c)** voltage.
14. **(c)** 30 × 2/3 = 20 V.
15. **(b)** in 10 A = out (I1 + 3) → I1 = 7 A.
16. **(b)** KVL, energy.
17. **(d)** 12/3 = 4 Ω.
18. **(c)** diode.
19. **(b)** diode.
20. **(b)** infinite.

</details>
