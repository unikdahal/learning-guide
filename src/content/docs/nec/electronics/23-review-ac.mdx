**How to use:** 20 questions, 20 minutes, no notes. Target **16/20**. Then, for Lok Sewa, write a 5-mark answer to "Explain series resonance with its characteristics" in 8 minutes (use the table in [lesson 20](/learning-guide/nec/electronics/20-resonance/)).

1. 10 µF and 15 µF in series give:
   (a) 25 µF (b) 6 µF (c) 150 µF (d) 12.5 µF
2. Energy stored in a 20 µF capacitor at 200 V is:
   (a) 0.4 J (b) 0.8 J (c) 4 J (d) 2 mJ
3. Inductors in series combine like:
   (a) capacitors in series (b) resistors in series (c) resistors in parallel (d) they can't be combined
4. A coil's current changes by 2 A in 10 ms and the induced voltage is 40 V. Its inductance is:
   (a) 0.2 H (b) 2 H (c) 20 H (d) 0.02 H
5. R = 100 kΩ, C = 10 µF. Time to (practically) charge fully:
   (a) 1 s (b) 5 s (c) 10 s (d) 0.5 s
6. Just after switching, a de-energised inductor behaves as:
   (a) a short circuit (b) an open circuit (c) a capacitor (d) a resistor of R
7. `v = 200 sin(314t)`. Its RMS value is about:
   (a) 200 V (b) 141 V (c) 127 V (d) 100 V
8. The frequency of the wave in Q7 is:
   (a) 314 Hz (b) 100 Hz (c) 50 Hz (d) 60 Hz
9. The half-cycle average of a sine wave of peak 100 V is:
   (a) 0 V (b) 63.7 V (c) 70.7 V (d) 50 V
10. The peak factor of a sine wave is:
    (a) 1.11 (b) 1.414 (c) 1.57 (d) 1
11. Inductive reactance at 0 Hz (DC) is:
    (a) infinite (b) zero (c) equal to R (d) 2πL
12. In a pure inductor, current:
    (a) leads voltage by 90° (b) lags voltage by 90° (c) is in phase (d) lags by 45°
13. R = 8 Ω, XL = 6 Ω in series. The power factor is:
    (a) 0.6 lagging (b) 0.8 lagging (c) 0.8 leading (d) 0.75
14. L = 1 mH, C = 1 µF. The resonant frequency is about:
    (a) 5 kHz (b) 1 kHz (c) 159 Hz (d) 50 Hz
15. At parallel resonance, the line current is:
    (a) maximum (b) minimum (c) zero always (d) Q times the supply current
16. A series RLC circuit has Q = 20 at f0 = 10 kHz. Its bandwidth is:
    (a) 200 kHz (b) 500 Hz (c) 50 Hz (d) 20 kHz
17. P = 3 kW, Q = 4 kVAR. Apparent power S is:
    (a) 7 kVA (b) 5 kVA (c) 1 kVA (d) 12 kVA
18. A lagging power factor is corrected using:
    (a) series inductors (b) parallel capacitors (c) series resistors (d) parallel inductors
19. In a delta connection, the line voltage is:
    (a) √3 × phase voltage (b) equal to phase voltage (c) phase voltage/√3 (d) 3 × phase voltage
20. Total power of a balanced three-phase load with VL = 400 V, IL = 5 A, pf = 1:
    (a) 2 kW (b) 3.46 kW (c) 6 kW (d) 1.15 kW
