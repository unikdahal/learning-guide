This course covers **NEC 7.5–7.6** and **Lok Sewa Computer Engineer unit 10 (operating systems)**; for Computer Officer, OS is about **10%** of Paper II.

<Aside type="note" title="What the exam actually asks">
OS questions are mostly **numericals with fixed procedures** — Gantt charts and average waiting time, Banker's safety algorithm, page-fault counts, address translation, effective access time, disk head movement — plus a few **standard written answers** (process states, deadlock conditions, paging vs segmentation, semaphores). The labs reproduce the numericals so you can check every hand calculation.
</Aside>
