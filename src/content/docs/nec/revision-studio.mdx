Plan from your [syllabus checklist](/learning-guide/nec/study-hub/), retrieve without notes, solve fresh [practice questions](/learning-guide/nec/practice/), then write an answer. This studio keeps those activities together while preserving the distinction between actual quiz answers and your own confidence ratings.

## Use this alongside the full syllabus

The recall deck samples one original question from each NEC sub-section. A card is a retrieval cue, not a complete lesson or evidence of mastery. CE/CO filters use the same editorial overlap map as the preparation desk; they do not include every PSC-only requirement. Use the [Lok Sewa preparation hub](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/) for those additions and the [source register](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/source-register/) for official samples and historical-question verification limits.

Your checklist confidence, quiz history and recall self-ratings remain separate. A strong self-rating never silently turns an incorrect quiz answer into a correct one. Written self-checks are learning aids and do not predict official marks.
