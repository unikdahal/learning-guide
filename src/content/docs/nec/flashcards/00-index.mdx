The plan keeps **Friday night for flashcards**: no new material, just fast recall of the week's formulas and facts. Each deck below matches one week of Phase A.

<Aside type="tip" title="The method (15–30 minutes)">
1. Read the question, **say or write the answer before revealing it** — recognising an answer is not the same as recalling it.
2. Mark honestly: **Knew it** removes the card; **Again** sends it to the back of the round.
3. Go round until the deck is empty. Progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and resume.
4. Before each mock (Sat 12 Dec, Sat 26 Dec, Wed 30 Dec, Fri 1 Jan), press **Start over** on the decks for your weakest chapters.
</Aside>
