This course teaches **NEC Chapter 5 (ACtE05, about 10 questions)** and, at the same time, **Lok Sewa Computer Engineer unit 1 (computer networks)** plus **15.9 cryptography and network security**. For Computer Officer it is the **networking 10%** of Paper II.

<Aside type="note" title="Where you start from">
You use HTTP, DNS, TLS and sockets every day at work, so the top layers will feel familiar. The marks are in the parts software engineers rarely touch: **subnetting, CRC and Hamming codes, sliding-window efficiency, CSMA/CD frame sizes, IP fragmentation, Dijkstra tables and RSA by hand** — and in writing protocol comparisons as structured answers. Those get the most space here.
</Aside>

## How to study each lesson (45–60 minutes)

1. Read once.
2. Do every worked numerical yourself on paper before reading the solution.
3. Use the lab — predict, then check.
4. Write the Lok Sewa answer timed (8 min per 5 marks), then compare with the model.
5. MCQs; log every miss.
