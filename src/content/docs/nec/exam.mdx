## The paper

| Item | Value | Source | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Questions | 100 MCQs, 1 mark each, four options | NEC's own ACtE model question, headed "100*1 = 100" | Confirmed (official) |
| Change from older papers | Earlier sittings used 60 × 1 + 20 × 2; a notice of Bhadra 17, 2082 moved to 100 × 1 | Edusanjal, summarising the NEC notice | Likely |
| Duration | 2 hours | Edusanjal and OpenExamPrep, citing NEC's 2082 procedure | Likely — check admit card |
| Pass mark | 50% | Same | Likely — check admit card |
| Negative marking | None reported | Edusanjal lists it as N/A | Unconfirmed — check admit card |
| Mode | Conflicting reports: paper-based vs computer-based | Edusanjal says paper; a prep blog says CBT | Unknown — check admit card |
| Weighting | 10 questions per chapter, at least one from each of the 6 sub-chapters | Syllabus rules as summarised by Edusanjal | Likely |

<Aside type="note" title="The official model paper is not exactly 10 per chapter">
Sorting NEC's model paper by chapter gives roughly: Ch1 9, Ch2 10, Ch3 11, Ch4 11, Ch5 12, Ch6 10, Ch7 8, Ch8 11, Ch9 12, Ch10 6. Treat "10 each" as the target, not a guarantee, and don't skip any chapter.
</Aside>

**If there is no negative marking, answer every question.** Do two passes: everything you know cold first, then the marked ones.

| Date | What | Source |

| Tue 17 Nov 2026 | Registration opens at [register.nec.gov.np](https://register.nec.gov.np/) | Your notes — check the portal |

| On the admit card | Your exact day, shift, centre and reporting time | Admit card |

Fees reported by a third-party guide: **NPR 2,500** exam fee (double if late) and **NPR 5,000** registration fee after passing. Confirm both on the portal.

NEC exam support: 9767472423 (Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00) or exam_support@nec.gov.np, as listed on the registration portal.

## Still to confirm

1. Exact day and shift of the Computer Engineering paper inside the January window.
2. Duration, negative marking and paper vs computer mode.
3. That your KIIT degree plus the equivalence certificate is accepted at registration with no extra step.

## Exam-day rules

- No new material the night before. Re-read the formula sheet and chapter "know cold" lists only.
- Exams run in the daytime, Nepal time (15 minutes ahead of India). Start moving your sleep earlier around 20 Dec; the study plan has the steps.
- Carry the admit card, citizenship certificate (original), pens, and anything else the admit card lists.
