## Do not memorise an ambiguous stem as a universal rule

In the same model, **Q2** leaves the quantity being measured unclear, **Q21** offers more than one questionable DMA-mode label, and **Q29** combines mail submission and retrieval. Treat these as prompts to state assumptions, not reasons to invent a definitive official key.

For studying the underlying concepts:

- Maximum-power transfer and efficiency are different quantities. For a resistive Thevenin source, `Pload = Vth² RL / (Rth + RL)²` is largest at `RL = Rth`. At that point the series source resistance dissipates equal power, so the **efficiency of this resistive model** is 50%. That is not a general statement that only half of any source’s possible output is transferable.
- DMA transfers can use burst, cycle-stealing or transparent access arrangements. Interrupts may announce completion; that does not make every interrupt-related label a distinct standard transfer mode. Always check the stated processor/controller terminology.
- SMTP handles mail transfer/submission; POP3 and IMAP support mailbox retrieval/access. A question spanning both operations needs more precise wording than a question about just one operation.

<Aside type="tip" title="Your answer log needs two columns">
Record **what the source actually asks** separately from **the principle you can defend**. If a teacher or unofficial key disagrees, compare assumptions and derivations first. Do not label an editorial answer as NEC’s answer key.
</Aside>

## A source-led study session

1. Attempt a small block from the PDF without notes. Mark confidence before checking any answer.
2. Write down the question number and PDF page, your reasoning and any missing assumptions.
3. Use the [syllabus checklist](/learning-guide/nec/study-hub/) to find the matching sub-section.
4. Relearn the concept, then attempt a fresh [original question](/learning-guide/nec/practice/). A remembered letter is not evidence that you can transfer the idea.
5. For PSC overlap, write the same concept as a short explanation, diagram or numerical trace. Consult the [Computer Engineer map](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/computer-engineer/) for the actual written scope.

## What counts as a verified past-year question?

At minimum, retain the authority, exact post and level, year/session, paper, scan page and question number. Prefer the issuing authority’s archive. A third-party scan may still be useful, but record its host and whether the exam header, date and complete pages were inspected.

An undated typed list is a **practice collection**. A candidate’s memory is a **recall**. An authority’s sample is a **model**. A dated paper from a completed sitting is a **past paper**, with verification strength depending on the source. Keep these categories separate before counting repeats or comparing years.

No verified multi-year NEC historical corpus is bundled here. This register establishes a traceable starting point; it does not turn the original workbench into a PYQ database.
