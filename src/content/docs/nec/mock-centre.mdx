Practise across the NEC technical syllabus using **120 original learning MCQs**, two per section. These are editorial practice questions, **not verified past-year questions, an official model paper, or evidence of exam readiness**. The [question bank](/learning-guide/nec/practice/) identifies its syllabus source and question provenance.

Choose 20, 60 or 100 questions, your own duration, and either zero penalty or a **PSC-style −0.2 wrong-answer penalty**. This is **not a complete PSC mock**: general awareness and PSC-specific extras are missing. Personal timing and scoring are training choices, not claims about an official exam scheme. Consult the [PSC preparation hub](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/) and [source register](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/source-register/) for those requirements and official evidence.

## How saved rounds work

Change or clear answers and flag questions until you confirm submission or the absolute deadline arrives. Answers and explanations stay locked until then. Reloading resumes the saved round; time continues while the tab is closed or hidden. A finished round records each answered question once in existing practice history; blank answers add no attempts. A wrong-answer retry is a new attempt and can add new history when finished.

Only one current round/report is retained in this browser. Starting another round replaces it, while practice history remains. The seed reproduces selection and question order for the same round size and bank version. A retry contains only wrong answers and has no chapter-coverage guarantee. Browser storage can be cleared or edited; this is a personal learning tool, not a secure assessment. When storage is blocked, reload loses the in-memory session. If saving fails, keep the tab open and use “Retry saving history”.
