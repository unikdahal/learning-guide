## A repeatable 90-minute block

1. **10 minutes: retrieve.** Write yesterday’s definitions, formula assumptions or algorithm from memory. Check afterwards.
2. **30 minutes: learn one sub-section.** Work through the linked lesson. Draw the state transition, circuit, table or architecture yourself.
3. **20 minutes: solve.** Attempt fresh questions. Record the misconception behind each error, including lucky correct answers.
4. **20 minutes: write.** If relevant to PSC, explain one answer with a labelled diagram, trace or calculation. Answer the actual command verb: compare, derive, design or justify.
5. **10 minutes: close the loop.** Check the solution, update confidence, and choose the next block. A topic marked “needs review” comes back before new work.

The queue uses a simple, visible rule: review after **1 day** for “needs review”, **3 days** for “learning” and **7 days** for “recall ready”. These are study habits in this guide, not official exam rules or a validated mastery estimate. Changing only the written checkbox does not postpone recall review. After your next closed-book check, change confidence or press **Reviewed now** to record another check at the same level.

## Keep evidence beside each question

For a past question record the authority, post, year in its original calendar, paper, question number and scan page. Keep the source wording separate from your corrected interpretation. An official **model** paper is a sample paper, not proof that a question appeared in a previous sitting. Unverified recalls can suggest practice ideas; they cannot support claims about repeat frequency.

Official scope: [NEC ACtE syllabus](https://nec.gov.np/uploads/Syllabus-Registration/1763716654201_Syllabus%20Computer%20Engineering.pdf), [federal PSC Computer Engineer Paper I](https://psc.gov.np/site_uploads/course/20231110070747654dd6c388682.pdf), [Paper II](https://psc.gov.np/site_uploads/course/20231110070747654dd6c3886821.pdf). Check the syllabus attached to your advertisement before treating a provincial, local-level or public-enterprise paper as equivalent.
