The guide runs in seven parts. Each builds on the ones before it, and each module lists
what to read first. Levels go from **beginner**, which assumes only programming, to **expert**,
which assumes you could contribute to an engine.

Articles marked with an open ring in the sidebar are planned but not written yet. Their pages
already describe what they will cover.
