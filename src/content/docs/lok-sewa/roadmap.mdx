## The idea in one paragraph

Lok Sewa Paper II is 100 written marks in 3 hours across sections A (25), B (25), C (20) and D (30). **Units 1–4 and 6–10 — Sections A and B, about half of Paper II — overlap with the NEC licence exam**, so Phase 1 studies them at Lok Sewa depth while preparing for NEC. Phase 2 adds what NEC doesn't give you (Section C, unit 5, Section D, Paper I) and turns knowledge into **timed written answers**. Phase 3 is mocks and taper.

**Lok Sewa additions inside Phase 1** (small, so they don't hurt NEC):
- **Two timed answers a week** from the [answer bank](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/computer-engineer/answer-bank/), chosen at the Sunday review — about 20 by 20 Dec, ★ reported questions first.
- **Admin:** Computer Officer application (plan date Wed 25 Nov) and the PSC regular deadline (plan date Tue 1 Dec) — check both on the official site; settle the open **NEC-licence eligibility question** for Miscellaneous Service posts with PSC before the deadline.
- **General knowledge:** the NEC plan's Sunday GK slots, 30 minutes — current affairs, constitution, geography.

**Exit check (3 Jan):** every course's review test done; about 25 timed answers written; error log in hand.

## Phase 2 · after your NEC exam – 12 Mar · Lok Sewa only

Nine weeks. Rest for two or three nights after the NEC exam, then:

| Week | Main block (weeknights) | Weekend |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · 11–17 Jan | **Unit 5** electronic communications: analog and digital communication systems, AM/FM/PM, sampling and PCM, ASK/FSK/PSK/QAM *(to build)* | Section D: technical-writing piece 1 · case study 1 (redo the [worked one](/learning-guide/nec/se/06-case-study/) from memory) |
| 2 · 18–24 Jan | Unit 5: SNR, BER, Shannon capacity, multiplexing · **Unit 13** compiler design: phases, lexical analysis, grammars *(to build)* | Paper II Section A timed set (units 1–5) |
| 3 · 25–31 Jan | Unit 13: top-down and bottom-up parsing, SDT, intermediate code, runtime storage, optimisation | Technical writing 2 · case study 2 (school graduation DFD) |
| 4 · 1–7 Feb | **Section C at Lok Sewa depth**: unit 11 AI (search, game playing, planning, NLP), unit 12 theory of computation extras (closure, pumping lemma, pigeonhole, undecidability, P vs NP) | Section B timed set (units 6–10) |
| 5 · 8–14 Feb | Unit 14 graphics extras (raster algorithms, transforms, projections, hidden surface, shading) · **unit 15** electives you choose to bank on (cryptography, distributed computing, e-commerce, embedded already done) | Technical writing 3 · case study 3 |
| 6 · 15–21 Feb | **Computer Officer extras**: IT in Nepal (IT policy, Electronic Transactions Act, Copyright Act, Unicode), e-commerce and e-governance, MIS/ERP/DSS, computer fundamentals | Section C timed set |
| 7 · 22–28 Feb | **Paper I Part II** MCQ practice across units 1–15; Paper I Part I reasoning sets | **Full Paper II mock 1** (3 h, all sections) |
| 8 · 1–7 Mar | Repair: weakest unit from mock 1 · error log | **Full Paper II mock 2** · Paper I mock |
| 9 · 8–12 Mar | Taper: ★ answers again, flashcards, formulae, Nepal IT facts | Light review; pack |

**Every night, all through Phase 2:** 20 minutes of general awareness and reasoning for Paper I Part I (it has negative marking — practise skipping).

**Targets for Phase 2:** at least **60 more timed answers** (every unit, Section A and B units twice); **6 technical-writing pieces** and **4 case studies**; **2 full Paper II mocks** at or above 60/100 on your own honest marking.

## Phase 3 · 13–19 March · exams

- **Sat 13:** travel. **Sun 14:** rest; read only the ★ answers and your error log.
- **Mon 15 – Tue 16:** Computer Officer papers (if applied). Evenings: 30 minutes of Computer Engineer Section D notes, then sleep.
- **Wed 17 – Thu 18:** Computer Engineer Paper I and Paper II.
- After results: possible Computer Officer practical test, document verification, **group test (10)** and **board interview (30)** — prepare for those once results are out.
