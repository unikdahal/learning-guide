## Exam scheme

| Stage | Paper | Marks | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Written | Paper I, Part I — general awareness (25) + general ability/reasoning (25) | 50 | MCQ, negative marking for wrong answers (verify the rate) |
| Written | Paper I, Part II — general technical: Section A 34%, B 36%, C 30% of units 1–15 | 50 | MCQ per your draft (a provincial copy shows a written format — verify) |
| Written | Paper II — technical subject, Sections A 25, B 25, C 20, D 30 | 100 | Written, 3 h |
| Final | Group test (leaderless discussion) 10 + board interview 30 | 40 | — |

## Syllabus and NEC coverage

**Course** = a full course on this site teaches the unit at Lok Sewa depth, with timed model answers (all collected in the [answer bank](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/computer-engineer/answer-bank/)). **Partial** = the NEC chapter page covers the core; extras are listed at its foot. **New** = not in NEC; planned for Phase 2 (January–March).

## What NEC doesn't give you

Plan these for Phase 2 (4 Jan – 12 Mar):

1. **Unit 5 Electronic communications** — modulation (AM, FM, PM; ASK, FSK, PSK, QAM), sampling and PCM, SNR, BER, Shannon capacity.
2. **Unit 13 Compiler design** — whole unit.
3. **Unit 15 emerging-tech items** not yet covered — simulation and random-number tests, speech and image processing, multimedia, e-commerce, expert systems, GIS/remote sensing/GPS, adaptive web, high-speed networks.
4. **Section D** — weekly practice: one technical-writing piece and one case study (method and a full worked answer in [SE lesson 6](/learning-guide/nec/se/06-case-study/)).
5. **Paper I Part I** — general awareness and reasoning.
6. The **Partial** extras for units 11, 12 and 14, listed at the foot of NEC chapters 6 and 9.

Units 1–4 and 6–10 are now taught as full courses during Phase A of the [NEC plan](/learning-guide/nec/roadmap/), so from January the work there is **written practice** from the [answer bank](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/computer-engineer/answer-bank/), not new study.

**NEC chapter 10** (drawing, economics, NEC Act) is NEC-only, apart from feasibility and budgeting in Section D.
