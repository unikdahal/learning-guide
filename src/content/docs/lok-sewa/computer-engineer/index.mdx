## Exam scheme

| Stage | Paper | Marks | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Written | Paper I, Part I — general awareness (25) + general ability/reasoning (25) | 50 | MCQ, negative marking for wrong answers (verify the rate) |
| Written | Paper I, Part II — general technical: Section A 34%, B 36%, C 30% of units 1–15 | 50 | MCQ per your draft (a provincial copy shows a written format — verify) |
| Written | Paper II — technical subject, Sections A 25, B 25, C 20, D 30 | 100 | Written, 3 h |
| Final | Group test (leaderless discussion) 10 + board interview 30 | 40 | — |

## Syllabus and NEC coverage

**Covered** = NEC notes already teach it (you still need to practise writing it). **Partial** = NEC covers the core; the extras are listed on that NEC chapter page. **New** = not in NEC; study it from January.

**Paper I, Part I** (general awareness and reasoning, 50 marks) has no NEC overlap at all.

## What NEC doesn't give you

Plan these for Phase 2 (4 Jan – 12 Mar):

1. **Unit 5 Electronic communications** — modulation (AM, FM, PM; ASK, FSK, PSK, QAM), sampling and PCM, SNR, BER, Shannon capacity.
2. **Unit 13 Compiler design** — whole unit.
3. **Unit 15 emerging-tech items** not in NEC — simulation and random-number tests, distributed objects, image processing, multimedia, GIS/GPS, adaptive web, high-speed networks.
4. **Section D** — weekly technical-writing and case-study practice (DFD, ER, use case, sequence diagrams, feasibility).
5. **Paper I Part I** — general awareness and reasoning.
6. The **Partial** extras listed at the bottom of each NEC chapter page.

**NEC chapter 10** (drawing, economics, NEC Act) is NEC-only, apart from feasibility and budgeting in Section D.
