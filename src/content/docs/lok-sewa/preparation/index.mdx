**Goal:** know exactly what the two exams look like, from PSC's own syllabus PDFs, and how each hour of NEC licence study feeds them.

<Aside type="note" title="What changed after checking the official PDFs (17 Nov 2026)">
- **Computer Engineer Paper I, Part II is MCQ**: 50 questions × 1 mark. (The CE syllabus page flagged this as "verify".)
- **Every wrong MCQ answer costs 20% of that question's mark**; a blank costs nothing. No calculator in MCQ papers.
- **CE Paper II** is 2 × 5 + 7 × 10 + 1 × 20 marks in 3 hours, with a **separate answer book for each section**.
- **All Computer Officer unit weights are printed in the official PDF**, so the "†" ones on the CO page are confirmed, and so are the CO Paper II section marks (30 / 20 / 20 / 30).
- **Laws and policies are examined as in force three months before the exam date.**
- **No Computer Engineer or Computer Officer past paper was found in the checked PSC question-bank index**: that search covered 89 listed papers on 17 Nov 2026. This does not establish absence from every official archive. See the [source register](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/source-register/).
</Aside>

## The two exams, side by side

Both posts are Nepal Miscellaneous Service, Gazetted Third Class, open competition. Both syllabi took effect on **2080/07/20 BS** and repealed the earlier ones.

| | Computer Engineer | Computer Officer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper I (100, pass 40) | Part I general awareness and reasoning, 50 MCQ × 1 · Part II general technical, 50 MCQ × 1 · **1 h 30 min for the whole paper** | same format |
| Paper I Part II weights | Section A 34%, B 36%, C 30% (units 1–15) | 12 units with printed weights (DBMS 14%, most others 10%) |
| Paper II (100, pass 40, 3 h) | 2 × 5 + 7 × 10 + 1 × 20; Sections A 25%, B 25%, C 20%, D 30% | 10 × 10; Sections A 30, B 20, C 20, D 30 |
| Section D / special | 16.1 technical writing (1 × 10) + 16.2 case study (1 × 20) | none; networking + six smaller units share Section D |
| Practical test | — | **50 marks, pass 25, 1 h 30 min, 5 × 10** (OS 1, DBMS 2, programming 1, networking 1) |
| Group test | leaderless group discussion, 10 marks, 30 min | same |
| Interview | board, 30 marks | same |
| Total | 200 written + 40 | 200 written + 90 |

For Computer Officer, **only candidates who pass the practical** go on the final merit list, even though the merit order uses the combined total.

## What the rules mean for how you answer

- **MCQ maths.** With four options and a 20% penalty, a pure guess is worth 0.25 × 1 − 0.75 × 0.2 = **+0.10 marks**. Once you can rule out one option, a guess is worth 0.333 − 0.133 = **+0.20**. Under these assumptions a guess has positive expected marks, including a blind guess. That is an expectation, not a guaranteed gain. More generally, an attempt has positive expectation when your probability of being right exceeds 1/6. Check the actual number of options and penalty in the paper instructions.
- **No calculator** in Paper I. Practise the [number-system](/learning-guide/nec/digital/01-number-systems/) and [subnetting](/learning-guide/nec/networks/04-ip-subnetting/) shortcuts by hand.
- **Paper II answer books are per section.** An answer written in the wrong section's book may not be marked. Before writing, check which section the question is in.
- **"Two or more parts" is official.** A 10-mark question can be 2 + 8 or 5 + 5, or two short notes. Mirror the split in your answer: one heading per part, and length in proportion to the marks.
- **Laws are those in force three months before the exam.** For Computer Officer unit 12 (IT in Nepal) and any Computer Engineer technical-writing answer that cites policy, use the text current at that cut-off.

## Time budget for Paper II

Three hours for 100 marks is **1.8 minutes per mark**. Keep about 15 minutes in reserve for reading, planning and checking, which leaves about **1.6 minutes per mark**. That gives the time tags used in every review page: 5 marks · 8 min, 10 marks · 16 min, 20 marks · 32 min.

| Computer Engineer Paper II block | Marks | Minutes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the whole paper; tick the questions you know best | — | 8 |
| 2 short (5-mark) answers | 10 | 16 |
| 6 long (10-mark) answers in Sections A–C | 60 | 96 |
| Section D technical writing (10) | 10 | 16 |
| Section D case study (20) | 20 | 32 |
| Check diagrams, labels, units | — | 12 |
| **Total** | 100 | 180 |

PSC does not say how the 10 questions split across Sections A, B and C. Only Section D's 10 + 20 is fixed. One split consistent with the 25/25/20 weights is 5 + 10 + 10, 5 + 10 + 10 and 10 + 10, but this is an inference, not a rule.

## Evidence labels

Every question or exam claim in these pages carries one of these labels. The [source register](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/source-register/) records the URLs and verification limits behind them. A longer editorial evidence log is retained with the project source.

| Label | Meaning | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Official** | read directly in a PSC or NEC document | marks, timings, rules, syllabus wording |
| **Official sample** | sample question printed inside the PSC syllabus | the best guide to question style; not a past paper |
| **Transcription** | typed copy on a third-party site, no scan or provenance | practice and topic hints; wording may differ from the real paper |
| **Reported (unverified)** | earlier notes in this guide, no source found | practice only |
| **Original practice** | written for this guide | practice only |
