## Scope and evidence

The workstream labels are shortened editorial groupings derived from the federal [Computer Engineer Paper I](https://psc.gov.np/site_uploads/course/20231110070747654dd6c388682.pdf) and [Paper II](https://psc.gov.np/site_uploads/course/20231110070747654dd6c3886821.pdf), and [Computer Officer Paper I](https://psc.gov.np/site_uploads/course/20231110070338654dd5cad7373.pdf) and [Paper II](https://psc.gov.np/site_uploads/course/20231110070338654dd5cad73731.pdf). They complement the shared NEC core rather than enumerating every syllabus bullet. Use the syllabus for your exact advertisement and the [source register](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/source-register/) before making historical-question claims.

Reference notes are personal entries. A check date records what you entered, not a verification performed by this website. For volatile facts, retain the document version and exam cut-off in your notes.
