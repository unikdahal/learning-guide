## 2. Transcriptions: CE 2078 and CE 2079

Source: a third-party study blog, as typed text, with no scan, date, advertisement number, or account of how it was obtained ([2078](https://nextgenacademyyy.blogspot.com/p/subject-computer-engineer-subjective.html), [2079](https://nextgenacademyyy.blogspot.com/p/computer-engineer-subjective-2079.html), read 17 Nov 2026). The "reported CE 2078/2079" items elsewhere in this guide match these texts.

**Why to treat them with care:**
- Both sittings were set under the **previous syllabus**. The current one took effect on 2080/07/20 and repealed it.
- Each section totals correctly (25/25/20/30), but some section placements don't fit the current syllabus. 2078 puts *pseudo-random numbers and the gap test* (now unit 15.1, Section C) in Section A. 2079 puts *knowledge representation* (now AI, Section C) in Section A and asks "OOP over structured programming" twice (Q4 and Q6). Either the old syllabus was sectioned differently or the transcription has errors; there is no way to tell which.
- Two questions in 2079 (Q3, Q9) have no marks printed. The section totals imply 10 each.

### What the two transcriptions suggest

Two sittings are too few for statistics, but the pattern fits the official Section D wording and is worth acting on:

- **Section D is predictable in shape.** Technical writing was a *concept note* (2078) and a *views* essay on a current issue (2079). The case study was a Nepal public-service scenario asking for process lists, a DFD and relationships (2078), then feasibility, use case, sequence and ER (2079).
- **Questions are definitional plus explanation**, split as 2 + 3, 2 + 8 or 3 + 7. The definition carries few marks; the explanation, diagram or example carries most of them.
- **One numerical per paper** (subnetting in 2078). Plan for one each from scheduling, subnetting, normalisation and a tree/heap operation.
- **Electronics, digital design, communications, compiler, graphics and ToC numericals did not appear in either paper.** Do not read that as "safe to skip": the current syllabus gives Section A 25% and lists units 3–5 explicitly.

## 4. Original practice

Every question written for this guide is labelled **Original practice** where it appears. They are modelled on the official samples and the transcriptions' style, and are not claimed to be from any exam. The largest sets are in the [workshop](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/written-answer-workshop/#10-original-practice-set), the [CE gap units](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/ce-gap-units/) and the [CO page](/learning-guide/lok-sewa/preparation/co-non-overlap/).

## How to keep this register honest

1. If you find a scan of a real paper (PSC website, a candidate's photo with the PSC header and date), record it in a **Scan** tier with its host, header, date and completeness. Use **Official** only when the issuing authority publishes or authenticates it.
2. Never upgrade a transcription because several sites repeat it. Most copy each other.
3. Re-check the PSC question bank before the exam, and re-download the syllabus to make sure it hasn't been revised.
