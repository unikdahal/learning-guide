## Translation glitches noticed (not used for practice items)

- Rules 27 and 28 cite "clause (e) of s18(1)". Section 18(1) has only (a)–(c); the intended clause is presumably (c).
- Rule 23 says "inquiry committee"; Rule 20 says "investigation committee".
- Rule 3A(2), on eligibility for the professional-engineer examination, is hard to parse in English. The lesson states only what the r2(d) definition and Schedule 2A support.
- The ministry is named differently in Act s36 (Physical Infrastructure) and Rule 38 (Physical Planning and Construction). Not tested.
- This record does not settle which language text prevails in law. The lesson tells readers to check the Nepali text.

## Editorial choices

- The drawing, risk, financing, contract and ethics cases are original and use hypothetical numbers. They are not past-paper questions.
- **First- and third-angle symbol.** Derived from two facts: the end view shows two solid circles, so it is the small end of the cone; and each method has its own placement rule for that view. Cross-checked against Wikipedia's ISO 5456-2 description: in first angle the trapezoid's short side points away from the circles; in third angle it points toward them. ISO 5456-2 itself is paywalled and was not read.
- **Sheet sizes, scale and isometric factor.** The ISO 216 sheet sizes repeat Chapter 10. The isometric factor is derived mathematically as the square root of 2/3. ISO 128, ISO 5455 and ISO 216 were named, not fetched.
- The OHS hierarchy of controls, the risk EMV method, capital structure and the contract-type risk allocation are standard textbook concepts, presented as general principles and not as Nepal-specific law.
